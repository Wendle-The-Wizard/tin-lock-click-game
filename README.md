# Tin Lock Click Game

I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard, and this is a little game about trying my brass key on the cupboards in the tower. Only one lock is the right one.

This is the last step (5 of 5). The key follows your pointer, the game checks which lock it fits, the right lock gives a soft click, and now the cupboard that opens glows warmly with a few sparks.

To play, open the page and move the mouse, or tap on a phone. Line the key tip up with a keyhole until its ring turns yellow, then click or tap. The line under the picture tells you what happened. Turn your sound on to hear the click.

I added a Sound button so you can switch the click on or off and hear a test click, and a New game button to hide the secret somewhere new.
