# Tin Lock Click Game

I'm Wendle, a tiny apprentice wizard, and this is a little game about trying my brass key on the cupboards in the tower. Only one lock is the right one.

Right now (step 4 of 5) the key follows your pointer, the game checks which lock it fits, and the right lock gives a soft click sound. Move the key tip over a keyhole and its plate gets a bright ring, then click or tap to try it. A line under the picture tells you what happened. The glow is coming next.

To play, open the page and move the mouse, or tap on a phone. The key jumps to where you tap. Turn your sound on to hear the click.

I also fixed a sneaky bug: I had named a variable "top", and the browser already uses that name, so my cupboards vanished!
